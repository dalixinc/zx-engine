Ctrl is the Spectrum's Symbol Shift. Shoot fish, bombs and missiles for points; touching anything, or the black ground, costs a life. Runs in JSSpeccy 3 (GPL-3.0, licence). For a real Spectrum or another emulator: atlantis.tap.
