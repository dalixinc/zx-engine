; diver.txt - player sprite, 24 x 15, drawn FACING RIGHT.
;   #  = ink   -  = paper (opaque, e.g. helmet glass)   .  = transparent
; Swimming: helmet at the front, air tank on his back, flippers behind,
; and the jet unit he holds out in front (torpedoes will come out of the
; dark mouth at its nose). Frames: a flipper kick, with the jet unit's
; propeller (at its back end) changing between frames.
;
; The propeller used to be one ink pixel that only touched the jet on a
; diagonal (frames 0 and 2). The kick order is 0,1,2,1, and frame 1 does
; not have that pixel, so while he is moving the speck blinks on and off
; beside the jet. At 1:1 on a Spectrum that reads as dirt left behind him.
; It is part of the sprite, not a hole in the save/restore (that path was
; checked: after erase, the playfield matches the backdrop). The blade is
; now joined onto the jet, so it cannot sit there on its own.
;
; Kick, drawn to read at 1:1 (fins are at least 2 pixels thick, and every
; ink pixel shares a full edge with another — a diagonal touch disappears
; on a real screen):
;   frame 0  fins apart. Upper fin on the left of rows 9-10, lower fin on
;            rows 13-14, with an empty gap between them. Both reach column 0.
;   frame 1  half way. One thick fin, tip at column 2, notch not a gap.
;   frame 2  crossed and tucked. The fins make an X under the body and
;            stay to the right of column 4, so they no longer stick out.
