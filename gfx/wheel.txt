; wheel.txt - the pulsar bomb, the first Trigon.
;
; Drawn from assets/bombs/pulsar.png. His strip is two shapes,
; alternating: the wide cap and the narrow cap. Eight frames of
; that strobe. The ink sits in a 12 by 12 box
; inside his 16 by 16 cell, and that box is what is stored.
; The pulse was the caps and side pixels changing, and was hard to see.
; Now it is two shapes that differ a lot: the burst, his wide shape with
; four rays flashing out at the corners and the bars joining up top and
; bottom, and the ring, drawn in from the box's edge with no caps at
; all. Held two steps each, burst, ring, burst, ring, so it pulses twice
; a cycle (WHEEL_SPIN, 8 1/50 seconds a step: about 0.6 s a pulse).
; Still two stored pictures, still 12 by 12: no bytes, no time.
; mirror off: it is the same either way.
